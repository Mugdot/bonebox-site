EVERY BOSS IS A BOX

You shoot your way through the dungeon. Then the boss door opens, the screen shatters, and none of that works any more: it is you, a box, and a pattern to survive, turn by turn, until you can hit back.

Coming to Steam Windows. Keyboard or controller. Solo, co-op or versus.
300

playable characters. Each one is a skull wearing one thing, with its own weapon, its own special and its own story.

20

dungeons and not one of them is a forest. A mouth. A candle crypt. The inside of a clock. That is what the menu says, anyway.

6872

image files. Every sprite and every frame of animation, hand drawn by Melaxia.

The dungeon

Each level is a safe hub with rooms branching off it. Doors lock when you walk in and open when the room is clear. Clear a room for an item, beat a mini-boss for a pick of power-ups, and open the portal down.

The box

The fifth level of every dungeon ends at a boss door. Behind it the game changes: you steer a soul through the boss's attacks inside a box, then take your turn. Fight, or use an item.

With someone

Play a whole run in co-op with a friend or an AI partner. Certain pairs of characters share a bond, and a bond changes what your hits do. Or settle it in versus.

Nobody has found it yet

There are secrets on the title screen, in the walls, and in at least one place the menu does not admit to. We are not saying how.
