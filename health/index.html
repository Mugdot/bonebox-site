HEALTH WARNING

Please read this before you play, and before you let a child play.

Photosensitive seizure warning

A very small number of people may have a seizure when they see certain visual images, including flashing lights or patterns that can appear in video games. This can happen to people who have no history of seizures or epilepsy.

BONEBOX contains flashing lights, rapid changes of brightness, screen-wide flashes, screen shake and fast-moving patterns. Boss fights and special attacks are where these are strongest.

Stop playing at once and see a doctor if you or your child have any of these while playing:

  • light-headedness, dizziness or altered vision
  • eye or face twitching, or jerking of the arms or legs
  • disorientation, confusion or a brief loss of awareness
  • any involuntary movement or convulsion

If you or anyone in your family has ever had a seizure or has epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing.

Turning the flashing down

The game has settings for this. From the title screen or the pause menu, open Options:

  • Flashes lowers the strength of full-screen flashes, all the way to off. Turning it down also slows the blinking of attack warnings.
  • Screen shake lowers how much the screen moves on impacts, all the way to off.

These settings reduce flashing and shaking. They do not remove every fast-changing image from the game, and they are not a guarantee that it is safe for someone with photosensitive epilepsy.

Playing more safely

  • Play in a well-lit room and sit back from the screen.
  • Do not play when you are tired or have not slept.
  • Take a break of ten to fifteen minutes every hour.
  • Stop if your hands, wrists, arms or eyes become tired or sore, and rest before you play again. If the soreness keeps coming back, see a doctor.

What is in the game

BONEBOX is a fantasy action game about skeleton characters fighting monsters. It has cartoon violence with no blood or gore, and mild spooky imagery: skulls, crypts and the like.
