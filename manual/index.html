ONLINE MANUAL

Everything you need for a first run. Every key and gamepad button can be changed in the game under Options, Controls.

How a run works

Pick a character and you start in the first level of a dungeon. A level is a hub (safe) with side rooms branching off it.

  • Side rooms lock behind you. Clear every enemy and the doors open. A cleared room leaves you an item.
  • Mini-boss rooms sit at the ends of the arms. Beat one and you choose one of three power-ups.
  • When every room on the level is clear, a portal opens in the hub and takes you down a level.
  • The fifth level of each dungeon holds the boss door. The boss fight happens in the box (below).

Beat five dungeons and the run is won. You will be asked whether you want to keep going. There are twenty dungeons, visited in a different order each run.

If you lose all your hearts the run ends. What you unlocked along the way stays unlocked.

Controls

These are the defaults on keyboard. On a gamepad you move with the left stick or d-pad and attack by pushing the right stick in a direction; buttons are listed in the game's own How To Play screen.

Boss fights: the box

Walk through the boss door and the fight moves into a box. It alternates between the boss's turn and yours.

The boss's turn

You steer a small soul inside the box and avoid everything that is not you. Every attack shows a warning before it can hurt. In some attacks the soul turns blue and gravity switches on: you stand on ledges and jump instead of floating.

Your turn

  • Fight. A marker sweeps across a bar. Stop it in the centre for a PERFECT hit. How hard you hit depends on the build you brought into the room.
  • Item. Use a pocket item.

Late in a big boss fight a single skill check appears. Do what it asks, quickly.

Characters

Every character has a weapon that plays differently, an EX attack, a Super EX, and power-ups and items that only they can find. Characters come in six classes: Warriors, Marksmen, Mages, Summoners, Tricksters and Guardians. On character select, the panel shows hearts, power, speed, reach and a difficulty rating from one to five stars.

Most characters start locked. The select screen tells you what each one wants from you.

Playing together

Co-op runs the whole game for two players. One player hosts and shares a room code; the other joins with it. You can also bring an AI partner. Some pairs of characters share a bond, shown on the select screen, which lends each of you a small extra effect.

Versus puts two players in an arena under a choice of rules.

Online play connects the two players directly. See the privacy policy for what that involves.

Options worth knowing about

  • Flashes and Screen shake can both be turned down or off. See the health warning.
  • Text speed changes how fast dialogue types out.
  • Controls lets you rebind every key and gamepad button.
  • Swap sticks and Stick deadzone are there if you hold a pad the other way round, or your stick drifts.

Saving

The game saves by itself on your own device: your unlocks, your records, your settings and a checkpoint at the start of each level so you can continue a run later. Nothing is uploaded.

Something is wrong

If the game misbehaves, tell us on the forum page. Say which character you were playing and what you were doing at the time.
