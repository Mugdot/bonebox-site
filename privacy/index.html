PRIVACY POLICY

The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your save stays on your device.

Effective 12 November 2026

This policy covers the game BONEBOX and this website. Both are made and run by Mugdot, an independent developer ("we", "us"). It explains what information is involved when you play or visit, and what is not.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. BONEBOX has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code. It does not ask for your name, email address, age or location, and it does not send us anything about how you play.

Your save file

The game saves your progress, unlocks, records and settings on your own device only.

  • In the desktop version, the save is stored in the game's own folder in your user profile on your computer.
  • In a web browser, the save is kept in that browser's local storage for the site the game is served from.

We never receive it and cannot see it. Uninstalling the game, deleting its data folder, or clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Online multiplayer

Single-player uses no network connection. If you choose to host or join an online co-op or versus game, the two players' devices connect directly to each other. To make that connection work:

  • The game contacts a public connection service (the PeerJS broker) so the two devices can find each other using the room code, and standard public STUN and TURN servers (operated by Google, Cloudflare and PeerJS) to get through home routers. Like any server you connect to, these services see your IP address. They are run by third parties under their own policies, not by us.
  • Because the connection is direct, the other player's device can see your IP address, and yours can see theirs. Only share a room code with people you are happy to connect to.
  • During the session the two devices exchange game data: which characters were picked, inputs, and the state of the game. This is not recorded by us and is not kept after the session ends.

There is no text or voice chat in the game.

Steam

If you get the game through Steam, your Steam account, purchase and any Steam features are handled by Valve under Valve's own privacy policy. We do not receive your Steam account details from the game.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third-party servers.

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may log technical information such as your IP address when you request a page, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The stats page can show your own records by reading the game's save from your browser's local storage. That happens entirely on your device; nothing is transmitted.
  • The forum page links to a public board on GitHub. If you post there you do so with your own GitHub account, under GitHub's terms, and what you post is public.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Online play connects players directly, so a parent or guardian should decide whether a child may use it and with whom.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to give you a copy of, correct or delete. If you think we hold information about you, contact us and we will check and tell you.

Changes

If the game or this site starts handling information differently, this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the game's public board on GitHub. Please do not put personal information in a public post.
